The Exploner approach

Exploner AI Turns Intent Into Action.

Instead of relying only on text instructions or static prerecorded tutorials, Exploner provides real-time contextual visual demonstrations and interactive experiences that respond to the user's objective, immediate question, and exact point of confusion.

Exploner virtual-lab technology

A Laboratory That Responds to the Learner

Exploner's virtual lab combines adaptive experimental environments with a 3D AI lab technician that can teach, demonstrate, and perform experiments during a live interactive session.

01

3D AI Lab Technician

A 3D laboratory technician can demonstrate, explain, teach, and perform live laboratory experiments while guiding the user through each procedure.

02

Live Questions and Interaction

Users can interact with the AI technician during the experiment, ask questions, request clarification, and ask for a procedure or concept to be demonstrated again.

03

Adaptive Experimental Setups

Exploner's virtual lab is not limited to static experimental setups. The environment and setup can be made to fit the user's objectives, learning needs, questions, and intended experiment.

04

Learning Through Participation

Users are not limited to passively watching an experiment. The experience supports exploration, guided practice, live questioning, and active participation throughout the session.

Interactive during live sessions

Not Just a Video. A Responsive Experience.

Exploner AI remains interactive throughout a live session. The user can ask questions at any point, request additional explanations, seek clarification, explore an alternative method, or ask the AI to repeat a demonstration.

1 Ask questions while a demonstration is happening.
2 Request clarification at the exact point of confusion.
3 Ask Exploner to repeat, adjust, or expand a demonstration.
4 Explore different approaches based on the user's objective.

What drives Exploner

Mission and Vision

Exploner is being developed to make practical learning more accessible and to reduce the barriers between a person's intent and ability to act.

Mission

Make practical learning accessible.

To make practical learning accessible to everyone by turning questions, goals, and curiosity into interactive experiences that help people understand, practice, and accomplish more.

Vision

Make almost anything learnable.

To build a world where anyone, anywhere, can learn how to do almost anything through an intelligent, interactive guide without being held back by a lack of expertise, equipment, time, or access to traditional instruction.
